TTSFreeUse reads documents aloud: PDF, Word (DOCX), and text files, or text you paste in. It's made for people who can't read print or find reading hard.

Free and private
- Everything runs on this computer. Your documents are never uploaded.
- No account, no subscription, no usage limits.

First start
- The voice (about 330 MB) downloads once, the first time TTSFreeUse starts.
- Saving MP3 or M4B audio downloads a free audio converter (FFmpeg, about 110 MB) the first time.
- TTSFreeUse opens in your web browser. While it's running, its icon sits by the clock; right-click it to quit.

Please use documents you have the right to use
Your own files, materials from your employer, school, or training program, books you've bought, and public-domain works. TTSFreeUse refuses password- and copy-protected files, and audio made from copyrighted works shouldn't be shared.
